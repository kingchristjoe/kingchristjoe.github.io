Research

Understanding the hydrogeochemistry of groundwater quality in Wassa East area of Ghana: implications for health risk assessment

Frontiers in Water | 2026

  • Investigated groundwater quality across the Wassa East area of Ghana using hydrochemical, statistical and spatial approaches.
  • Analysed groundwater from 139 boreholes, including physicochemical parameters, major ions, trace metals, cyanide and microbiological indicators.
  • Used Piper, Chadha, Schoeller, Gibbs and Gaillardet diagrams to characterise groundwater chemistry and hydrochemical processes.
  • Conducted data cleaning, statistical analysis and Principal Component Analysis (PCA) to identify hydrogeochemical processes and relationships.
  • Applied USEPA-based human health risk assessment models to evaluate carcinogenic and non-carcinogenic risks.
  • Applied the Integrated Water Quality Index (IWQI) using entropy and CRITIC weighting techniques to classify groundwater quality.
  • Developed groundwater quality maps using GIS and inverse distance weighting to examine spatial patterns in water quality.
  • Applied data storytelling techniques to communicate research findings through literature review, results and discussion.
