Research

Groundwater Hydrogeochemistry in Wassa East, Ghana

My research has focused on understanding groundwater chemistry, water–rock interactions and water-quality conditions in the Wassa East area of Ghana.


  • Characterisation of groundwater hydrogeochemistry.
  • Assessment of major ions and trace-metal contamination.
  • Evaluation of groundwater quality using water-quality indices.
  • Human-health risk assessment associated with selected contaminants.
  • Application of GIS and spatial interpolation to visualise groundwater-quality patterns.
  • Multivariate analysis using PCA, factor analysis and clustering.
